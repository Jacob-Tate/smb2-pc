SMB2 - license and credits
==========================

SMB2 (called "SMB2 PC Port" before version 1.0.4) is an unofficial,
non-commercial fan PC port of Super Monkey Ball 2. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by SEGA, Nintendo or any of their
affiliates. "Super Monkey Ball" is a trademark of SEGA.

This package contains NO game content. To play, you need your own copy of
Super Monkey Ball 2 for the Nintendo GameCube (USA release, disc ID GM2E8P)
as a disc image (.iso, .gcm or .rvz) that you made from a disc you own. During setup
the game data is extracted from YOUR disc image onto this computer only.
The game icon shown after setup is also generated from your own disc image.

The port and the decompilation it is built on are released under the
CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication. They come with NO WARRANTY of
any kind: THE SOFTWARE IS PROVIDED "AS IS", WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND,
EXPRESS OR IMPLIED. Use it at your own risk.
